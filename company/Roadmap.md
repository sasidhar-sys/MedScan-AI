# MedScan AI Product Roadmap

- 🟢 **Smart Esophageal Diagnosis System (SEDS)** - Early Esophageal Cancer Detection (Active Flagship)
- 🫁 **Smart Lung Diagnosis System (SLDS)** - Early Pulmonary Nodule & Lesion Analysis
- 👄 **Smart Oral Cancer Diagnosis System (SOCDS)** - Oral Lesion Classification
- 🩺 **Smart Skin Lesion Diagnosis System (SSLDS)** - Melanoma & Dermatological Screening
- 🧠 **Smart Brain Tumor Diagnosis System (SBTDS)** - MRI Segmentation & Classification
- 🎗 **Smart Breast Cancer Diagnosis System (SBCDS)** - Mammography & Ultrasound AI
- 🫀 **Smart Liver Disease Diagnosis System (SLDDS)** - Hepatic Pathology Screening
- 👁 **Smart Retinal Diagnosis System (SRDS)** - Diabetic Retinopathy & Ophthalmic AI
- 🌐 **Universal Clinical Decision Support Platform** - Unified Multi-organ CDSS Hub
